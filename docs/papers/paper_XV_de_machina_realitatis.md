## Abstract

We formalize the **MESIE Reality Engine** — ten modular **cores** that each host twenty **design-language intelligence agents**, bound by forty **enveloped protocols** and a three-layer **front ↔ middle ↔ back** stack. Born from video-game roots and aimed at Unreal-class product showcase on the open web, each core is a forgeable enterprise: libraries, templates, engine manifests, and Latin-named agents that route to native MESIE engines without third-party inference. The middle layer encodes design briefs via ST-φ; the back layer invokes polyglot compute and SOLUS receipts; the front layer renders Three.js, dashboards, micro-frontends, and XR shells. HTTP surfaces on `:8750` expose `GET /processor/reality/status` and `POST /processor/reality/invoke`, federated through enterprise multi-user envelopes.
